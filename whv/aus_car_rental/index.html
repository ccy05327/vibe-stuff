🏆 Top Provider Tiers

Major International Brands

Includes Avis, Hertz, Europcar, Sixt, Budget. Best for newer vehicles, extensive networks (airports & cities), and comprehensive service. Often have higher base prices but frequent loyalty deals.

Affordable & Local Options

Includes East Coast Car Rentals, Apex, No Birds. Excellent for budget-conscious travellers. May have slightly older vehicles, off-airport locations requiring a shuttle, or more restrictive mileage policies on certain deals.

💡 Essential Rental Tips

  • Book in Advance: Prices increase significantly closer to the rental date, especially during holidays.
  • Understand Insurance: The default excess is high ($5000+). Reducing it costs extra per day but provides peace of mind. Check if your travel insurance or credit card offers rental car cover.
  • Age Restrictions: Drivers under 25 usually face a daily surcharge. Some companies won't rent to drivers under 21.
  • Check Mileage Limits: While often unlimited for standard cars, some budget deals or long-term rentals may have daily caps (e.g., 100-200km/day).
  • Inspect the Vehicle: Before driving away, thoroughly inspect the car for any pre-existing damage and ensure it's noted on your rental agreement.
