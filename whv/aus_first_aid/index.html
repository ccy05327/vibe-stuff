This guide focuses on the standard Australian First Aid certificate, "HLTAID011 - Provide First Aid". Most providers offer a 'blended' model, which is the most common and convenient option. This involves completing the theory component online at your own pace before attending a shorter, face-to-face session for the mandatory practical assessments. The information below reflects this popular blended format.

Key Information

📜

Certificate Code

The national standard for this qualification is HLTAID011 - Provide First Aid. Ensure any course you book covers this unit.

🗓️

Certificate Validity

Your First Aid certificate is valid for three years. The CPR component (HLTAID009) should be renewed annually.

📚

Typical Content

Courses cover CPR, bandaging, managing bleeding, burns, fractures, allergic reactions, and responding to common emergencies.
